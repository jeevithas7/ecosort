How this works

The demo uses MobileNet (a lightweight CNN) running in your browser with TensorFlow.js. Its predictions are mapped to six waste classes: plastic, paper, metal, glass, organic and e-waste. For the full project, we fine-tune the model with transfer learning on TrashNet and TACO images.
